About us

Organics Food began in 2016 with one question from a family kitchen: where does this vegetable actually come from?

We started by buying from five farmers near our town. Today we work with over 120 farms, and every one of them grows without synthetic pesticides, chemical fertilisers or GMO seed.

We keep our supply chain short on purpose. Fewer hands means fresher food, and a clear answer when you ask where it was grown.

Our promises

• Nothing sold unless it is certified organic.
• Plastic-free packaging wherever possible.
• Refund or replacement if you are not satisfied.
